- **Q1. [M0] The spec file was named `stonewake-spec.md`, but CLAUDE.md calls `SPEC.md` the source of truth.** — Chosen default: renamed the file to `SPEC.md`; content untouched. — Reason: CLAUDE.md is the standing order and references `SPEC.md §9/§10` by name. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q2. [M0] SPEC says "8 weapons" (§A10, and CLAUDE.md M4) but the §4.2 conversion table only yields 7 soul weapons (Palisade / Beacon / Sprout give passives, not weapons).** — Chosen default: 8 weapons = the 7 soul weapons + **Warden's Arrow**, an innate `slotless` weapon every Warden carries into Nightfall. It never consumes one of the 6 slots, so §4.1's "if more than 6 types qualify, pick 6" still bites at exactly 7 candidates. CLAUDE.md's M2 gate ("weapons from towers #2/#5 + Arrow") reads the same way. A4 is therefore tested as *7 attacking tower types + walls* = 8 single-type builds. — Reason: keeps every stated count true and guarantees a minimum-tower rush build has non-zero Act II damage, which A8 needs in order to measure a ≤25% win rate rather than a flat 0%. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q3. [M0] Map geometry: SPEC §2.3 fixes 36×20, 3 gates and a 2×2 Core "near the east-center" but not exact coordinates.** — Chosen default: gates at west (0,10), north (18,0), east (35,17); Core footprint (25,9)–(26,10). — Reason: keeps all three gate→Core paths 12+ tiles long so mazing has room, and spaces the east gate away from the Core. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q4. [M0] SPEC §9.1 forbids `Math.random`/`Date.now` but says nothing about `Math.sin`/`cos`/`atan2`/`hypot`, which are implementation-defined in ECMAScript and would break cross-engine replay hashes.** — Chosen default: `/src/sim/math.ts` provides `dsin`/`dcos`/`datan2` built from `+ - * /` and `Math.sqrt` only (all IEEE-754-exact); the sim never calls native trig. — Reason: A11 promises 100/100 reproducibility, which native trig cannot guarantee. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q5. [M0] What happens if the Warden dies during Act I? SPEC only defines Core death as the Act I failure state.** — Chosen default: the Warden reforms at the Core after taking lethal damage, at 50% Max HP with 2 s of invulnerability. No run-ending consequence. — Reason: §13.1 insists Act I stakes live on the Core; a second death condition would change that. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q6. [M0] Enemy damage against structures has no rate in the spec (only "enemies attack blocking walls").** — Chosen default: structure DPS = `coreDamage × 3`, times the enemy's `structureDamageMul` (Gatebreaker 2×), in `data/waves.json` as `enemyStructureDpsFactor`. — Reason: puts a 300 HP Palisade at ~30 s against a lone Husk and ~0.5 s against the Gatebreaker, matching "smashes walls". — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q7. [M0] Act II contact damage has no rate ("Core-damage → contact damage").** — Chosen default: one contact hit per enemy per 0.4 s, at radius `enemy.radius + 0.45` (`contactInterval` / `contactPadding` in `data/spawns.json`). — Reason: at one hit per second, being surrounded was survivable indefinitely and A3 could not pass; 0.4 s makes the crowd itself the threat. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q8. [M2] SPEC §5.1's Act II overlay ("HP × 0.6") is relative to the wave-1 roster, which would make Nightfall fodder ~7× weaker than the wave-10 enemies the player just cleared** (Act I scales HP ×1.18^(wave−1) = 4.44× by wave 10). — Chosen default: the ×0.6 overlay multiplies the statline Act I *ended* on, i.e. `hp × 0.6 × 1.18^(waveCount−1)`, then the ⚖ per-minute ramp on top. — Reason: Nightfall is the climax; measured with the literal reading the horde could never reach the Warden at all and A3 was unreachable. Both numbers used are the spec's own. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q9. [M2] Pierce and blast damage scale linearly with horde size, which A5 forbids.** "Piercing Bolt — pierces all" took 86% of all damage in a 350-enemy fight; Mortar Lob's r2 blast was nearly as bad. — Chosen default: each successive target a pierce shot or a blast hits takes less (`pierceFalloff` / `aoeFalloff` in `data/weapons.json`; blasts pay full damage to the nearest few, then decay). "Pierces all" still holds — it just does not pay full price to all. — Reason: without it, one weapon dominates every build and A5 cannot pass. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q10. [M2] Act II gem lifetime is unspecified, and uncollected gems grew without bound** (16,000+ in a long run). — Chosen default: gems fade after 45 s (`gemLifetimeSeconds`) with a hard cap of 500 (`gemCap`), both in `data/spawns.json`. — Reason: bounds the entity count for A10 and makes XP something you move to collect, which is the point of A3. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q11. [M2] SPEC §5.1's director spends its whole 10 s budget at once, which gives a stationary Warden quiet windows to heal through.** — Chosen default: the same budget accrues continuously and is spent as it lands. Rift events stay bursts, since that is their design. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q12. [M3] The Sundering could leave the Heartstone sealed inside the player's own maze, making a stationary Warden unreachable — which SPEC A7 rules out ("mazing is strong, never absolute").** — Chosen default: the Core detonation also shatters up to 4 cheapest approach lanes from the arena rim to the pocket, breaking petrified terrain only where no gap exists. — Reason: extends SPEC §4's "guaranteed open arena pocket" to mean the pocket is actually reachable. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q13. [M3] The 6 weapon slots can never bind, because class locks cap any one class at 5 soul-granting tower types.** There are 7 soul towers; 3 are class signatures, so a class can build its own plus the 4 unlocked ones. SPEC §4.1's "if more than 6 types qualify, the player picks 6" and §13.3's "8 towers compete for 6 slots" therefore never trigger in v1. — Chosen default: keep 6 slots and keep the picker implemented and tested; it only engages with the **Deep Roots** keystone (−1 slot) or a future unlock that lifts class locks. Logged rather than "fixed" because changing either the slot count or the class locks is a spec revision. — Suggested owner options: (a) drop to 4 slots so the choice bites, (b) let every class build every tower with signatures merely discounted, or (c) accept that slots only matter with keystones. — (owner verdict: superseded — SPEC-FINAL removed the slot picker (§6) entirely; this question has no successor.)

- **Q14. [M3] Warden base stats were constants in code, which CLAUDE.md forbids ("all game content/tuning as JSON in /data").** — Chosen default: moved to `data/warden.json`, schema-validated, exposed as `stats.BASE`. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q15. - **Q16. - **Q17. [M4] Nightfall is decided in its first minute, which made Act II a coin flip rather than a test of the build.** — Chosen default: the director budget ramps in over a `warmupSeconds` window (75 s, starting at 30% strength) on top of SPEC §5.1's per-minute growth. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q18. - **Q29. [playtest 2] SPEC has no practice or dev mode, but testing the late game means playing eleven minutes to reach it.** — Chosen default: a **practice run** opted into at the Hub. - **Q31. - **Q32. [b002] SPEC-V2 D1 does not say what Escape should do while the Abandon Run confirm sub-screen is open.** — Chosen default: Escape always resumes the run (same as from the plain Paused card), rather than stepping back one level to the plain pause card the way the on-screen Cancel button does. ## SPEC-V3 (M17 audit — see MIGRATION.md for the evidence behind each)

- **Q38. M23 adds Swordsman and Plaguebringer as owner-specced kits, giving 5. — Reason: inventing 8 class kits to then flag them legacy is exactly the "agent proposes nothing beyond QUESTIONS.md notes" line V3 §6 draws. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q39. [M17/V3 §4–§5] V3 §5 says towers "contribute their per-type VS special", but §4's VS-special column is specced for 3 of 10 towers** (Arrow: none; Electric: wire grid; Poison: trail). The other 7 have neither an upgrade track nor a VS special. — Chosen default: M20 proposes upgrade tracks for the 7 (owner sign-off per §4) and proposes **no VS special** for them, so they are pure obstacles in VS waves until the owner specs one. — Reason: a VS special is a mechanic, not a number; proposing seven of them is design work V3 reserves to the owner. — (owner verdict: superseded — SPEC-FINAL §5.2 authors the VS-special column for all ten towers; the open half of this question has a successor there.)

- **Q40. [M17/V3 §2] Multiplicative stacking (gate C4) invalidates every tuned number in `/data`.** Six +10% sources currently give ×1.60 and will give ×1.77 (+10.6%); the gap widens with source count. — Chosen default: **do no balance tuning in M18–M19**, re-baseline every balance bound (A1, A4, A9, B1, B2) in one pass at M27, and mark any gate that fails in between with a reason rather than nudging its constant. — Reason: tuning against a stacking model that is about to change is throwaway work, and nudged constants hide real regressions. — (owner verdict: approved)

- **Q41. [M17/V3 §1] Gate A10 ("a full headless run sims in ≤5 s") is red at HEAD — 3836 / 6080 / 6267 ms — and V3 invalidates its premise a second time.** The 5 s constant was written for a single-cycle run; f001 made a run three cycles long, and V3 §1 makes it 18 TD + 6 VS waves. This is not a performance regression: the sim did not get slower, the run got longer. — Chosen default: **leave A10 red with this recorded reason** and re-baseline it at M22 when the run length is finally known, rather than moving the number now. Options for the owner: (a) re-baseline to a per-wave or per-sim-minute budget, which survives future run-shape changes; (b) keep a whole-run budget and set it after M22; (c) keep 5 s and treat run length as the thing that must fit. Default is (a) proposed at M22. — **Measured update (M19/m19a, 2026-08-25):** A10 **passes on this machine**, both at this commit (9.6 s for the file, every run under the 5 s budget) and at HEAD `6be4dab` in a clean worktree (14.1 s for the file, still passing). The M17 audit measured it red at 3836/6080/6267 ms. - **Q131. [fb003] VS level-up auto-pick — where does the toggle live, and what counts as "the highest-rank owned stat boon"?** Owner feedback `feature-auto-pick-boons` (BACKLOG.md fb003) asks for a toggle (settings + on-screen) that resolves level-up offers without pausing, preferring the highest-rank owned stat boon, else the first offered card, with the picks themselves as ordinary Commands. Two judgment calls. (1) **The toggle is a `RunConfig` field (`autoPickLevelUps`), not a `Settings` field, and mid-run flips go through a new `set_autopick` Command rather than direct World mutation from the UI.** `src/ui/settings.ts`'s own doc comment is explicit that `Settings` is "presentation and accessibility only — nothing here may change the simulation, so a replay is identical whatever these are set to"; auto-pick fails that bar outright, since it changes which boons a run ends up with. `RunConfig` already carries the same-shaped precedent (`practice`, `policy`) for a sim-affecting run-start choice, so the pre-run Hub checkbox (mirroring the existing "Practice run" toggle) writes there. The in-run HUD button (`#sw-autopick`, next to Ranges) doesn't mutate `w.cfg` directly — it queues `{k:'set_autopick', on}`, so a toggle flipped mid-play is itself in the input log and a replay reproduces the same picks, not just the same starting config. (2) **"Highest-rank owned" reads as "the offered boon whose current rank in `w.boonRanks` is highest and greater than zero," ties broken by offer order; "owned" excludes a boon at rank 0 (never taken) even though `buildOfferPool` would still offer it.** Implemented as `pickAutoOfferIndex` (`src/sim/progression.ts`), called from `openLevelUpIfPending` when the flag is on: it rolls the real offers (unchanged `rollOffers`, so the RNG stream and offer pool are identical to manual play), then applies the pick via `applyOffer` — the same function `takeOffer` itself calls after its `phase === 'levelup'` guard — directly in a `while` loop, rather than routing through `takeOffer` (which early-returns unless the phase is already `'levelup'`, never true under auto-pick) or through `takeOffer`'s recursive `openLevelUpIfPending` re-check. The loop drains every currently-pending level-up in one call — decrementing `pendingLevelUps`, resetting `w.offers`/`w.rerollsLeft` once at the end — so it reproduces `takeOffer`'s other effects exactly without ever setting `phase = 'levelup'`, satisfying "never pauses" without a multi-tick drain (correction logged after code-reviewer's Minor on this item caught the mismatch between this paragraph and the shipped code). An empty offer pool (every boon at max rank — the same edge case BACKLOG.md's b005 already tracks for the manual path) skips the pause entirely under auto-pick rather than reproducing b005's soft-lock, though b005 itself (the manual-mode dead end) is unfixed and out of this item's scope. — Reason: CLAUDE.md's rule 5 (choose and log rather than stall) plus architecture rule 3 (all player actions are sim Commands) — a Settings-shaped toggle would have been the simpler UI wire-up but would have violated the file's own stated invariant and made a toggle-on run's replay diverge from a hand-authored `RunConfig` lacking the field. — (owner verdict: pending)

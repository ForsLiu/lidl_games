- **Q1. [M0] The spec file was named `stonewake-spec.md`, but CLAUDE.md calls `SPEC.md` the source of truth.** — Chosen default: renamed the file to `SPEC.md`; content untouched. — Reason: CLAUDE.md is the standing order and references `SPEC.md §9/§10` by name. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q2. [M0] SPEC says "8 weapons" (§A10, and CLAUDE.md M4) but the §4.2 conversion table only yields 7 soul weapons (Palisade / Beacon / Sprout give passives, not weapons).** — Chosen default: 8 weapons = the 7 soul weapons + **Warden's Arrow**, an innate `slotless` weapon every Warden carries into Nightfall. It never consumes one of the 6 slots, so §4.1's "if more than 6 types qualify, pick 6" still bites at exactly 7 candidates. CLAUDE.md's M2 gate ("weapons from towers #2/#5 + Arrow") reads the same way. A4 is therefore tested as *7 attacking tower types + walls* = 8 single-type builds. — Reason: keeps every stated count true and guarantees a minimum-tower rush build has non-zero Act II damage, which A8 needs in order to measure a ≤25% win rate rather than a flat 0%. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q3. [M0] Map geometry: SPEC §2.3 fixes 36×20, 3 gates and a 2×2 Core "near the east-center" but not exact coordinates.** — Chosen default: gates at west (0,10), north (18,0), east (35,17); Core footprint (25,9)–(26,10). — Reason: keeps all three gate→Core paths 12+ tiles long so mazing has room, and spaces the east gate away from the Core. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q4. [M0] SPEC §9.1 forbids `Math.random`/`Date.now` but says nothing about `Math.sin`/`cos`/`atan2`/`hypot`, which are implementation-defined in ECMAScript and would break cross-engine replay hashes.** — Chosen default: `/src/sim/math.ts` provides `dsin`/`dcos`/`datan2` built from `+ - * /` and `Math.sqrt` only (all IEEE-754-exact); the sim never calls native trig. — Reason: A11 promises 100/100 reproducibility, which native trig cannot guarantee. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q5. [M0] What happens if the Warden dies during Act I? SPEC only defines Core death as the Act I failure state.** — Chosen default: the Warden reforms at the Core after taking lethal damage, at 50% Max HP with 2 s of invulnerability. No run-ending consequence. — Reason: §13.1 insists Act I stakes live on the Core; a second death condition would change that. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q6. [M0] Enemy damage against structures has no rate in the spec (only "enemies attack blocking walls").** — Chosen default: structure DPS = `coreDamage × 3`, times the enemy's `structureDamageMul` (Gatebreaker 2×), in `data/waves.json` as `enemyStructureDpsFactor`. — Reason: puts a 300 HP Palisade at ~30 s against a lone Husk and ~0.5 s against the Gatebreaker, matching "smashes walls". — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q7. [M0] Act II contact damage has no rate ("Core-damage → contact damage").** — Chosen default: one contact hit per enemy per 0.4 s, at radius `enemy.radius + 0.45` (`contactInterval` / `contactPadding` in `data/spawns.json`). — Reason: at one hit per second, being surrounded was survivable indefinitely and A3 could not pass; 0.4 s makes the crowd itself the threat. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q8. [M2] SPEC §5.1's Act II overlay ("HP × 0.6") is relative to the wave-1 roster, which would make Nightfall fodder ~7× weaker than the wave-10 enemies the player just cleared** (Act I scales HP ×1.18^(wave−1) = 4.44× by wave 10). — Chosen default: the ×0.6 overlay multiplies the statline Act I *ended* on, i.e. `hp × 0.6 × 1.18^(waveCount−1)`, then the ⚖ per-minute ramp on top. — Reason: Nightfall is the climax; measured with the literal reading the horde could never reach the Warden at all and A3 was unreachable. Both numbers used are the spec's own. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q9. [M2] Pierce and blast damage scale linearly with horde size, which A5 forbids.** "Piercing Bolt — pierces all" took 86% of all damage in a 350-enemy fight; Mortar Lob's r2 blast was nearly as bad. — Chosen default: each successive target a pierce shot or a blast hits takes less (`pierceFalloff` / `aoeFalloff` in `data/weapons.json`; blasts pay full damage to the nearest few, then decay). "Pierces all" still holds — it just does not pay full price to all. — Reason: without it, one weapon dominates every build and A5 cannot pass. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q10. [M2] Act II gem lifetime is unspecified, and uncollected gems grew without bound** (16,000+ in a long run). — Chosen default: gems fade after 45 s (`gemLifetimeSeconds`) with a hard cap of 500 (`gemCap`), both in `data/spawns.json`. — Reason: bounds the entity count for A10 and makes XP something you move to collect, which is the point of A3. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q11. [M2] SPEC §5.1's director spends its whole 10 s budget at once, which gives a stationary Warden quiet windows to heal through.** — Chosen default: the same budget accrues continuously and is spent as it lands. Rift events stay bursts, since that is their design. — (owner verdict: pending)

## SPEC-V3 (M17 audit — see MIGRATION.md for the evidence behind each)

- **Q38. [M17/V3 §6] V3 says "other 9 classes: keep current kits, flag `legacy: true`", but `data/classes.json` has 3 classes (engineer, pyromancer, frost_warden), not 11.** The 11-class roster is a SPEC-V2 §2 aspiration that was never built; V2's B3/B5 gates reference it too. — Chosen default: flag the **3 existing** classes `legacy: true` and treat the other 8 as unbuilt content, not as legacy kits. M23 adds Swordsman and Plaguebringer as owner-specced kits, giving 5. — Reason: inventing 8 class kits to then flag them legacy is exactly the "agent proposes nothing beyond QUESTIONS.md notes" line V3 §6 draws. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q39. [M17/V3 §4–§5] V3 §5 says towers "contribute their per-type VS special", but §4's VS-special column is specced for 3 of 10 towers** (Arrow: none; Electric: wire grid; Poison: trail). The other 7 have neither an upgrade track nor a VS special. — Chosen default: M20 proposes upgrade tracks for the 7 (owner sign-off per §4) and proposes **no VS special** for them, so they are pure obstacles in VS waves until the owner specs one. — Reason: a VS special is a mechanic, not a number; proposing seven of them is design work V3 reserves to the owner. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q40. [M17/V3 §2] Multiplicative stacking (gate C4) invalidates every tuned number in `/data`.** Six +10% sources currently give ×1.60 and will give ×1.77 (+10.6%); the gap widens with source count. — Chosen default: **do no balance tuning in M18–M19**, re-baseline every balance bound (A1, A4, A9, B1, B2) in one pass at M27, and mark any gate that fails in between with a reason rather than nudging its constant. — Reason: tuning against a stacking model that is about to change is throwaway work, and nudged constants hide real regressions. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q41. [M17/V3 §1] Gate A10 ("a full headless run sims in ≤5 s") is red at HEAD — 3836 / 6080 / 6267 ms — and V3 invalidates its premise a second time.** The 5 s constant was written for a single-cycle run; f001 made a run three cycles long, and V3 §1 makes it 18 TD + 6 VS waves. This is not a performance regression: the sim did not get slower, the run got longer. — Chosen default: **leave A10 red with this recorded reason** and re-baseline it at M22 when the run length is finally known, rather than moving the number now. Options for the owner: (a) re-baseline to a per-wave or per-sim-minute budget, which survives future run-shape changes; (b) keep a whole-run budget and set it after M22; (c) keep 5 s and treat run length as the thing that must fit. Default is (a) proposed at M22. — **Measured update (M19/m19a, 2026-08-25):** A10 **passes on this machine**, both at this commit (9.6 s for the file, every run under the 5 s budget) and at HEAD `6be4dab` in a clean worktree (14.1 s for the file, still passing). The M17 audit measured it red at 3836/6080/6267 ms. So the gate is not red *in the code* — it flips with the host, which is a stronger argument for Q41 option (a) than the one originally made: a wall-clock budget that passes or fails depending on which machine runs it does not measure the sim. PROGRESS.md's "A10 is red" line has been corrected to say so. — Reason: "how long may a full run take to simulate" is a budget the owner owns, and a gate whose constant is moved whenever it fails is not a gate. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q42. [M17/V3 §7–§8] 18 TD waves each granting 1 random equipment, drawn from a fixed 12-item table, guarantees duplicates.** No duplicate rule is stated. — Chosen default: duplicates are allowed and stack in the stash as separate items. — Reason: the stash already holds multiple instances, and re-rolling or converting duplicates is a new mechanic. — (owner verdict: pending — a "duplicate converts to a shard" style rule would be a natural v0.4 addition)

- **Q43. [M17/V3 §1] Multi-summon's early-call bonus is ambiguous when 3 waves are stacked.** The V1 rule pays `remaining build time × 2 gold/s`, i.e. a refund for time skipped; stacking three waves collapses three build phases into one, so the naive reading pays three full bonuses for one skipped phase. — Chosen default: pay the bonus **once per wave called, computed against that wave's own un-elapsed build time** — full bonus for the first, zero for the second and third of a stack, since their build phases had not started. — Reason: keeps the bonus a refund for time actually skipped, which is what makes it a risk/reward choice rather than free gold. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q44. [M17/V3 §2–§3] Burning's −1 armor/s stacking into uncapped negative armor scales quadratically.** 30 stacked applications = −90 armor = +90% normal damage taken, and each further stack keeps adding. V3 says this "exploits §2's uncapped negative armor", so it reads as intended. — Chosen default: implement as written, add a **floor of −100 armor ⚖** so damage taken cannot exceed ×2, and flag it for the M19 balance pass rather than pre-emptively nerfing it. — Reason: a stated intent should ship and be measured, not be quietly softened; a floor keeps it from being unbounded while preserving the effect. — (owner verdict: pending)

- **Q45. [M17/V3 §10 T5] The Tuner writes `/data/*.json`, so the content a replay was recorded against can change under it.** C6 asks for "determinism holds per config", implying a config identity that does not exist — `RunConfig` carries no content hash today. — Chosen default: add an FNV-1a **content hash** over the loaded `/data` JSON to `RunConfig` and into the end-state hash's inputs, so replaying against edited data fails loudly instead of diverging silently.
